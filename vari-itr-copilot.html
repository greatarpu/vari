Portal survival guide

The exact clicks on incometax.gov.in, including the screens designed by someone who was clearly having a bad day. Field-tested by an actual confused human.

Before you log in

Keep ready: Form 16, AIS, 26AS, and your Aadhaar-linked mobile for the final OTP. Confirm at least one bank account is pre-validated (Profile → My bank accounts) — mandatory even with no refund.

Also keep: coffee, patience, and mild optimism.

Start the return: ITR-1, online mode

e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return → AY 2026-27 → Online → Individual → ITR-1.

Unsure about ITR-1? The Which form am I? tab knows.

"Reason for filing" — pick the boring first option

Choose "Taxable income is more than basic exemption limit."

The exciting-looking second option (Seventh Proviso — ₹1 crore deposits, ₹2L foreign travel…) is for people below the taxable limit who must still file. If a salary hits your account every month, that's not your movie.

Your whole name is sitting in "Last Name" — leave it alone

PAN insists on a surname; single names get parked there. Half of Tamil Nadu is in this club. Everything matches on PAN, not name order. Do not start a PAN correction nine days before the deadline.

Nature of employment → "Others"

Private-sector employees pick Others. Yes — you spent years building a career and the government filed you under "Others". It has zero tax impact. Breathe.

The regime question — a double negative, because of course

"Do you wish to exercise the option u/s 115BAC(6) of opting out of new tax regime?"

No = stay in the new regime. Yes = go old. Read it twice, answer once. Get your answer from the stamp in the first tab.

Salaried folks can re-choose every year — but only if the return is filed by the due date.

Verify pre-filled salary and TDS

Gross salary should match Form 16 item 1(d); Schedule TDS1 should match the Form 16 total and 26AS (all rows with booking status F). Don't "fix" a mismatch by editing — a mismatch means a document is stale.

Add "Income from Other Sources" from your AIS

Savings + FD/RD interest + dividends. RD interest is fully taxable in both regimes — the RD doesn't care about your feelings. If the portal pre-fills less than AIS shows, add the difference.

Exempt income (Schedule EI) — the free stuff

Disclose here, tax-free: SSY interest (10(11A)), PPF interest (10(11)), post-office savings interest up to ₹3,500 (10(15)(i) — works even in the new regime). Ten seconds of honesty, zero rupees of tax.

Pay the balance — and the famous challan disappearing act

If tax > TDS: e-Pay Tax → Self-Assessment Tax (300) → AY 2026-27.

The trap: your freshly paid challan often refuses to appear in the return for 30 min–24 hrs. It's not lost — it's just being processed at government speed. Never pay twice. Check e-Pay Tax → Payment History; if paid, enter the challan manually in Tax Paid: BSR code, date, serial number, amount. CPC matches it later.

Submit, then e-verify IMMEDIATELY

An unverified return is legally a return that never happened — Schrödinger's ITR. Aadhaar OTP, same sitting, then download the ITR-V. Now go tell your family group you filed before them.

Deadline for salaried, AY 2026-27: 31 July 2026. Belated filing works till 31 December with a late fee up to ₹5,000 — and you lose the old-regime option for the year. The portal also mysteriously slows to bullock-cart speed on July 30–31. File early.

Which form am I?

Answer honestly for FY 2025-26 — the department already knows anyway. Any single "Yes" usually promotes you out of ITR-1.

ITR-1 (Sahaj) — "Sahaj" literally means easy, and for once the naming is accurate. File it online with pre-filled data.
MNC employees, one sec: RSUs/ESPP of a foreign parent (held on a foreign exchange) are foreign assets → ITR-2 with Schedule FA is mandatory, and skipping it invites penalties with many zeros. Business or freelance income moves you to ITR-3/ITR-4 — different quiz, different life.
